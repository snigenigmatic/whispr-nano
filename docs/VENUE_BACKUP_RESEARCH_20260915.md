# Publication backup routes for the Indian-English ASR capstone

**Checked:** 15 September 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). This is a deadline and fit check from official venue and publisher pages, not a prediction of acceptance. A submission receipt is not a publication; only an acceptance into an archival track produces the peer-reviewed publication the capstone needs.

## Recommendation

Use a three-level plan:

1. **Primary practical target — BHASHA at ICON 2026, short paper.** Submit by **15 October 2026 AoE**; notification is **10 November 2026**. The official 2026 site seeks peer-reviewed work on tools, benchmarks, resources, and evaluation metrics for Indian languages. Short papers may be four pages, and accepted papers that are presented will be included in the ACL Anthology. This timeline supplies a submission receipt for the 16 October review and a decision in November. The fit is promising for a careful Indian-English ASR evaluation study, but Indian English is not explicitly named in the scope: email the chairs now to confirm that Indian-accented English grouped by speakers' Indian native languages is eligible. The parent ICON site lists BHASHA as an official workshop and identifies its CMT track. [Official 2026 workshop site](https://bhasha-workshop.github.io/), [submission and publication details](https://bhasha-workshop.github.io/submission.html), [ICON workshop listing](https://www.icon2026.org/workshops)
2. **Ambitious target — COLING 2027 short paper.** Submit through the October ACL Rolling Review cycle by **12 October 2026**. COLING explicitly includes benchmarking/evaluation, language diversity, and speech recognition; short papers may be four pages. The official schedule gives **10 February 2027** as the acceptance notification date. This is the strongest main-conference route here that can yield a truthful “submitted” status before the 16 October capstone review. Submit only if the measurement-reliability contribution is complete and independently checked; the existing multi-system audit by itself has already been judged too close to prior work. [Official COLING call](https://2027.coling-iccl.org/calls/main_conference_papers/)
3. **Best safety net — EACL 2027 Student Research Workshop (SRW), archival research paper.** The direct-submission deadline is **15 December 2026** and acceptance notification is **5 January 2027**. The track accepts undergraduate first authors, completed or preliminary work, and explicitly encourages informative negative results. Speech and multimodality are in scope. Accepted archival papers enter the EACL proceedings. It also offers optional pre-submission mentorship: draft due **6 November**, feedback due **5 December**. This is the best fit if October is too early or the contribution remains a careful student study rather than a main-conference result. The page's ARR-commitment year appears internally inconsistent; use the direct-submission route and confirm the live OpenReview deadline before submitting. [Official EACL SRW call](https://2027.eacl.org/calls/srw/)

**Decision rule:** ask BHASHA for scope confirmation immediately, while preparing one ACL-format four-page paper that can serve either BHASHA or COLING. By 5 October, require a frozen claim, complete matched-cohort results, robustness checks, and a full draft. Submit to COLING by 12 October only if it meets the main-conference bar; otherwise submit the stronger revised paper to BHASHA by an internal **14 October** deadline. If BHASHA says Indian English is out of scope or the paper is still incomplete, use EACL SRW and its mentorship deadline. Never submit the same paper to BHASHA and COLING simultaneously: both prohibit overlapping archival review.

## What can be claimed at the 16 October review

- If the BHASHA upload is completed: **“Submitted to the peer-reviewed BHASHA Workshop at ICON 2026; decision expected 10 November 2026.”** It is still not “accepted” or “published.”
- If the COLING upload is completed by 12 October: **“Submitted to ACL Rolling Review for commitment to COLING 2027; decision expected 10 February 2027.”** It is still not “accepted” or “published.”
- If it is not ready: **“Target: EACL 2027 Student Research Workshop; pre-submission mentorship draft due 6 November; archival paper due 15 December; decision 5 January 2027.”**
- A public repository, demo, technical report, arXiv identifier, or submission receipt is useful proof of work. None is a peer-reviewed publication.

## Preprints are useful, but they do not solve the publication requirement

arXiv can establish a dated, citable public version and allow the work to be shared quickly. arXiv itself describes its model as sharing research “without peer review”; therefore an arXiv posting must be labeled **preprint**, never “published paper” or “accepted paper.” [Official arXiv annual report](https://info.arxiv.org/about/reports/2020_arXiv_annual_report.pdf)

IEEE's SigPort is another legitimate repository for signal-processing manuscripts and artifacts. Its official page explicitly distinguishes repository submissions from formal publications. [Official SigPort description](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/sigport)

Check the chosen venue's current preprint and anonymous-review policy immediately before posting. Do not submit the same paper simultaneously to archival venues whose policies prohibit dual submission.

## Bottom-line venue strategy

The practical route is **BHASHA submission by 15 October, after written scope confirmation, with EACL SRW as the committed safety net**. COLING is the higher-risk target if the paper reaches main-conference quality by 12 October. EACL Demo is a separate product-oriented opportunity only if a real installable audit system can be finished in the next week. IEEE Access should not be used as a paid emergency escape hatch: speed and an APC do not imply acceptance. No legitimate venue can guarantee a publication by paying or by choosing a shorter deadline.
