# Validated public Hindi pilot — 22 September 2026

For C. Kaustubh. Four H100 inference runs completed under the USD15 batch cap.
No training or fine-tuning was performed. All six result directories, including
previous Mac runs, pass independent data/hash/completeness/scoring validation.

## Results

Identical three public Hindi conversations, 43.9167 minutes, 89 fixed 30-second
non-overlapping windows, 6,503 reference words. Primary scores preserve repeated
text and include capped outputs. Lower WER/CER is better.

| Model and decoding | WER | CER | Capped windows |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Whisper Small, greedy (Mac) | 106.46% | 84.59% | 69/89 |
| Whisper Turbo, greedy (Mac) | 66.98% | 55.01% | 15/89 |
| Whisper Turbo, greedy (H100) | 66.98% | 55.01% | 15/89 |
| Whisper Turbo, beam-5 (H100) | 52.02% | 43.23% | 4/89 |
| Whisper Turbo, timestamps (H100) | 64.09% | 50.41% | 21/89 |
| Qwen3-ASR-1.7B, greedy (H100) | 24.59% | 20.23% | 0/89 |

The Mac and H100 Turbo greedy transcripts are exactly identical. Beam-5 reduces
Turbo WER by 14.962 percentage points and improves each conversation. Timestamp
decoding alone increases capped windows and is not the selected repair.

Qwen scores 26.22%, 18.26%, and 28.76% WER on the three conversations; all finish
without capping. Its official repetition cleanup changes zero of 89 windows,
so the raw-output and official-parser WER agree. This is a comparison of complete
model recipes: Whisper uses float32 and Qwen uses the documented BF16 inference
path. It is not an isolated architecture or precision ablation.

## What is fixed, and what is not

The original decoder suffered severe repetition. Beam search repairs much of it,
without training or changing the scorer. The audio preprocessing and source
pairing were independently reproduced exactly from pinned Parquet bytes.
Qwen establishes that this public cohort supports substantially better open-model
transcriptions than the original Whisper pilot.

These are descriptive development results from only three conversations. They
do not establish a new ASR method, statistical superiority on a population,
fairness across speaker groups, phone performance, or a publishable contribution
by themselves. The public release is not verified to be the exact BRIDGE test
set. No direct comparison against Humyn's closed-model leaderboard is justified.
SPIRE held-out data has not been opened or used for this repair.
