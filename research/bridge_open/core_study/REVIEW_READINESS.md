# What remains before submission

The GPU audit supplies a reproducible measurement artifact. It does not by itself establish a publishable contribution. A skeptical read of the manuscript against the cited prior work identified these remaining scientific gates:

1. **Reference and meaning review.** Obtain actual language-competent responses to the bounded packets, including independent overlap and adjudication. Keep uncertain/unjudgeable cases visible. Neither script counts nor WER can supply these judgments.
2. **A narrowly stated contribution.** The available-Svarah compact-model result is a descriptive case study. Svarah already benchmarked open and commercial systems, and Vimarsha supplies broader coverage and reviewed reference alternatives. A larger leaderboard is not the differentiator. The present artifact's contribution candidate is its auditable recipe/resource/failure analysis.
3. **Measurement limits.** Treat the frozen single-reference scorer as an evaluation convention. CER and complete edit counts help inspect its effects; they do not resolve alternate valid wording. Any later normalization sensitivity analysis must be declared secondary and retain the original result unchanged.
4. **External validity.** The available Svarah shard is partly pre-exposed and lacks verified speaker IDs. Do not extend its result to all Indian accents, unseen speakers or phone deployment. Three timing passes over twelve repeated clips quantify timing variation only. Complete source recovery, a separately frozen untouched cohort or device measurements would be needed for those broader claims.

The next action is to complete and interpret the human review, then decide whether the bounded case study meets a particular venue's contribution standard. No training run or further model expansion is required to finish this handoff. No submission, acceptance or publication is recorded by these files.
