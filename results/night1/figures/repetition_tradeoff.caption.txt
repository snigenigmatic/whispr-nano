Pooled WER improvement can hide damage to genuine repetition. Whisper Turbo HF beam5 on 100 five-repeat human-keyword controls: (a) change from raw WER, with paired speaker-bootstrap 95% intervals (4,000 draws, 68 source speakers); (b) utterances whose word-edit count increases. One decoded run per condition. Repeats are constructed, not natural conversation. The published cap is the one-character-per-20-ms duration baseline. Negative WER change is better; no novelty is claimed for that cap.
